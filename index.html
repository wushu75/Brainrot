How it works

Three signals, one honest number

Brainrot watches the domain of whatever tab is in front of you — nothing else — and turns a week of it into a score you can't argue with.

01 · Track

Active-tab time

Only the focused tab's domain is counted, on a self-correcting timer. Background tabs, a closed lid, or a sleeping browser never inflate your time.

02 · Classify

Feeds vs. deep work

Sites sort into high-brainrot feeds, focused deep-work, or neutral. YouTube counts as neutral — but Shorts count against you. Every list is editable.

03 · Score

Reverse it

A live score, a 7-day trend, your top time sinks, and a daily recovery plan that names your worst offender and gives you one concrete move.

No black box

The math is right here

The score measures intentional attention, not raw screen time. Neutral browsing is ignored on purpose. Every weight lives in one config file you can change.

# per day: H = high-brainrot seconds, L = deep-work seconds ratio = H / (H + L + 1) volume = 1 - exp(-Hmin / 120) # saturates — endless scroll ≈ capped raw = 100 * (0.6*ratio + 0.4*volume) rebate = 25 * (1 - exp(-Lmin / 90)) # focused time buys it back score = clamp(raw - rebate, 0, 100)

Questions

FAQ

What exactly is a Brainrot Score?
A single 0–100 number where higher is worse. It weighs time on high-brainrot feeds against your focused deep-work time, factors in how much you piled on, and gives points back for real focus. It's a measure of intentional attention, not total screen time.
Is it really free?
Yes — free and open source under the MIT license. No paid tier, no ads, no upsell, no "pro" version dangling the useful features.
Does it track everything I do?
No. It reads only the domain of your active tab (plus whether a YouTube URL is a Short). It never touches page contents, full URLs, history, cookies, or background tabs, and it stores nothing off your device.
Can I change what counts as brainrot?
Fully. The settings page lets you add sites to the high-brainrot, deep-work, or ignore lists, and every scoring weight lives in a single config file.
Which browsers work?
Any Chromium-based browser that supports Manifest V3 — Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, and Opera among them.
